Mission Statement

Our organization, aims to provide assistance and support to seniors and low-income families in need. We help with food, clothing, rental assistance, socializing, mental health, and any other problems or issues that they encounter.

Our mission is to let these individuals know that they are not alone, and that there is a community of people who care about their wellbeing.

What We Do

Our organization works tirelessly to provide assistance and support to seniors and low-income families in need. We are working with management and social workers of all Miami-Dade County's senior buildings and centers.

From food and clothing to rental assistance and mental health support, we offer a wide range of services to help individuals overcome their challenges and improve their quality of life.

We also work with the Miami Lighthouse of the Blind to assist seniors and children that are blind or have lost more than 75% of their vision.

  • Backpacks & Public School Supplies

  • Clothes & Shoes

  • Food Drives & Luncheons

  • Living & Rental Assistance

  • Fun & Entertainment

Latest News

We're proud to announce our partnership with Miami Lighthouse for the Blind! This collaboration will enhance our services by integrating Miami Lighthouse's expertise in assisting adults and children with low vision.

Key highlights include:

- Programs for adults on "Living with Low Vision" and fall prevention.

- Training for our staff on sighted guide techniques.

- An inclusive early learning environment at Miami Lighthouse Academy for children ages one through four.

This partnership, will enable us to offer these services both at our locations and at Miami Lighthouse. Together, we're making a bigger difference. Stay connected for more updates on this collaboration.
